# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and potential clients evaluating Mahmoud Younis for front-end and full-stack development roles. They come to assess technical skill depth, project quality, and professional fit — forming a first impression within seconds.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that communicates Mahmoud Younis's expertise as a Full Stack Developer with a front-end focus. Every section validates competence: clean UI proves front-end skill, well-described projects prove backend depth, and the overall experience builds trust.

## Brand Personality

Modern, realistic, calm. The tone is confident without being loud, polished without being sterile. Professional warmth — the interface of someone who knows their craft and doesn't need to shout about it.

## Anti-references

None specified.

## Design Principles

1. **Competence by demonstration** — Every visual choice should reinforce that the person behind this site builds production-grade software. No empty decoration.
2. **Calm confidence** — The interface earns attention rather than demanding it. Restrained color, generous whitespace, deliberate pacing.
3. **Show the range** — Front-end polish meets backend substance. The design should hint at both without privileging either.
4. **First-scan clarity** — A recruiter or client should understand who this developer is and what they do within 3 seconds of landing.
5. **Consistency as craftsmanship** — Every pattern repeats deliberately. Typography, spacing, and interaction follow a system, not improvisation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AAA. Sufficient color contrast, readable type at all sizes, focus indicators preserved. Respect reduced motion preferences.
